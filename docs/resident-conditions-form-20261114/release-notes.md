# Resident experience questionnaire

Resident experiences now ask the working-condition questions from Noa Varssano's “מתמחים מדברים על תנאים” survey: start/end of a non-duty workday, monthly duties, monthly gross salary in full ILS, duty calm, peer relations, senior relations, work/family balance and optional advice. Institution/specialty are supplied by the selected department. The original four 1–5 scale anchors are retained. No gender field or additional identifying information is collected.

The primary form includes an explicit experience year and an independently answered overall recommendation for the site's stars. Other existing admission/training/team questions are available in a collapsed optional section. Unanswered fields remain absent; no score is preselected or silently restored when switching roles or navigating steps. Zero duties is a valid value and disables/clears duty calm. Paired times, units, numeric bounds and non-future years are validated on the server. Salary is explicitly gross; it is not combined with net salary.

New answers are versioned in the existing submission's roleDetails JSON. Approval/verification, private evidence, signup receipts and viewer gates retain their existing paths. After approval the department's resident tab displays these condition metrics with a denominator and year for each metric; missing answers are excluded. Existing imported survey aggregates keep their own source and historical period. The carousel uses the reported experience year, and recommendation stars never average the four condition scales. No imported response, score, source switch or approval decision is altered.

Migration `202611140003_resident_conditions_questionnaire` only permits NULL in five optional rating columns in ReviewSubmission and Review. It does not rewrite existing rows. Overall recommendation remains required. Legacy resident clients and student/intern forms keep their existing validation requirements. Null-aware display/aggregation prevents missing optional ratings from becoming zero scores.

Validation artifacts: unit-tests.log, build.log, test-migration.json, integration.json and responsive form screenshots. The browser integration uses synthetic submissions only on the isolated branch, exercises pending/approved/public states, denies non-admin publication, checks exact values and year, confirms guest privacy, issues a signup receipt with proof and text, and cleans up its records. No external email is sent. Existing source/moderation byte-boundary tests remain unchanged and passing.

Release uses the existing immutable archive, staged deployment, provider/source comparison, smoke, additive migration with a restore snapshot, promotion and GitHub main synchronization. Provider IDs and final status are recorded after deployment.
